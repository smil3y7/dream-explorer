**Sašo Drobnič**

*dreamer, psychonaut, and researcher of altered states of consciousness*

Dreams, and the phenomena connected to them, have followed me since early childhood — an encounter with a "foreign being" (a non-physical energetic entity) around the age of two, and nightly excursions during my teenage years that I'd forget by morning, awakened in me a drive to understand the mysteries of consciousness and the world's unexplained phenomena.

Growing up, like most teenagers, I got lost in the traps of my own mind and endless battles with inner demons — fears, doubts, prejudices, and inherited views of the world. Then, through experimenting with various alternative healing techniques (Reiki, Theta Healing, Tai Ji Quan, Heart & Soul Healing, Reconnection), attending all kinds of experiential therapeutic workshops, and deep self-inquiry, I came to understand that reality is not only what's taught in schools and reported in the media — that it extends beyond what we can perceive with our physical senses. That realization deepened my curiosity even further.

I continued my journey by practicing techniques described in the books of Carlos Castaneda, and expanded on them by experimenting with various dreaming aids. After years of research, I came across a group of dreamers called the "Dreamhackers," with whom I've collaborated for years now, both in physical and dream realities. Through persistence, I found various "allies" and teachers, with whose help I keep refining my skills in dreaming and uncovering the many layers of the reality we live in.

In the process of researching altered states of consciousness and the transitions into them, I tested a wide range of techniques and aids. It soon became clear that dreaming was my most direct path into these states, so for the past decade I've devoted myself intensively to researching conscious dreaming and out-of-body experiences — and occasionally other altered-state phenomena — passing on the information and experience I gather to others.

I'm the author of the lucid dreaming guide "Dream Gates," available in both Slovenian and English, the creator of the Dream eXplorer dream journal, and today I continue this research through [Sentria](https://sentria-navy.vercel.app).
